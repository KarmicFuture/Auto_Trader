Why a hot dog, though?

Because a bad day is usually a pile of small things. Inbox. Traffic. A meeting that could have been a sentence. You cannot fix the pile. You can hold something warm.

We do this to make life a little easier for everyone in line — you, us, the person behind you who just needs lunch to be simple. We want people to have fun. We want to put fun back into ordinary afternoons in this ridiculous world. A $3 hot dog is how we start.

We’re tech people who never quit loving a cart, a bun, and a good mustard stain. The name is Empty Taco. The help is a hot dog. Come as you are.

  1. 01 Keep it kind and cheap. Three dollars for the dog. Five with chips and a drink. No upsell speech. No “have you downloaded our app.”
  2. 02 Show up with something hot. Your office, your yard, your friend’s porch. We hitch the cart and we come to you.
  3. 03 Leave the day a little better. Full. A bit messier. Maybe smiling. That is the whole point.
