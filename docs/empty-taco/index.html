Why we do this

Because life is already a group project with no instructions. We sell a $3 hot dog to make the next ten minutes easier for everyone in line — you, us, the person behind you who just wants something hot and honest.

We want to have fun. Not “brand fun.” Actual fun. The kind you remember from when a mixtape and a parking lot counted as a night out. We are here to implement fun in all lives in this ridiculous world. That’s the whole business plan. The rest is mustard.

Tech people who never grew out of it. A hitch on a regular car. A cart that shows up at your place if you ask. No tacos. Never were. The name is a dare we kept.

  1. 01 Make it cheap enough to be kind. Three dollars. Five if you want the chips and the drink. Nobody needs a lecture with lunch.
  2. 02 Show up. Steam on. Music too loud. Office park, backyard, brewery lot, Tuesday that feels like a Monday. We hitch the cart and we come to you.
  3. 03 Leave people lighter. Full, a little messy, maybe wearing the shirt. Fun installed. World still ridiculous. Better anyway.
